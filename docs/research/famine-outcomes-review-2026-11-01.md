# Review: Gemini prompt 12 (famine outcomes, minimum band size)

Report: Project file `Hunter-Gatherer Band Famine Dynamics.pdf` (20 pages, uploaded 2026-11-01). Reviewed 2026-11-01, start of spec Q14. Feeds: revs 25, 26, 33, 35, 36 (checked below; none change) and Q14 (scenario realism).

## Verdict
Usable for direction, not numbers. The case table is mostly real events with plausible figures (three spot-checked, two match, one conflicts). The synthesis numbers are not: the outcome shares (45/30/15/7/3%), the weekly mortality rates and the 20%/50% deficit thresholds have no source and read as the model's own construction. The report's strongest contribution is qualitative and agrees with rev 35: starving in place needs a total staple failure plus immobility (transport loss, winter, epidemic); dispersal to kin and merger into a host are the common endings.

## For Q14
A band that dissolves into a neighbour outside the drought is the realistic "died out"; a band that relocates intact to refuge land is the realistic "migrated". Both match the slice's mechanisms, so the test does not need perishing.
