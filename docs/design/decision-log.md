## Vision
A terrarium you poke: an observational god-view sandbox where bands of people develop, change the land and each other, and make history in unexpected, funny and exciting ways. You watch, read the history, zoom in, and nudge things to alter the course. Jordan's framing: "like a kid with an ant colony." It is not a game to win, and it is not a copy of Earth's timeline.

**Positioning (working label):** "a terrarium you poke" / emergent history sandbox. (Replaces "interactive prehistoric documentary + butterfly-effect sandbox", rev 4.)

**Realism stance:** plausible mechanisms (scarcity, hunting, skill decay, scalar stress, diet breadth), not Earth fidelity. No Earth dates, no Earth climate curve.

**Long-term vision:** from simple bands to a Roman-Empire-like civilization. Later eras plug in as new systems on the same core, not rewrites.

## Scope (v1)
- Arc: simple hunter-gatherer bands to the first farming villages, on one procedurally generated landmass. Time counts from year 0 of the world.
- Version 1 must be worth watching on its own.

## Core loop (v1)
Live nudge and watch. Counterfactual forks are a v1 stretch goal (see Decisions).

## Success test (v1)
"I trigger a drought and can read in the Legends why one band migrated and another died out." Stretch: "...fork the world" before the drought and compare outcomes. v1 passes without the fork.
Use this to judge every "should we add X?" question.

## Hard rules (write tests before sim code)
1. **Hash-based randomness** keyed on (seed, absolute season index, entity, purpose), never a shared sequential stream. Test: replaying the same seed and nudge log produces an identical world (state hash). Implemented: `sim-core/src/rng.ts`.
2. **No platform-dependent math in sim-core.** Lint-ban Math.random, trig/exp/pow/log, `**`, Date, performance.now; use own implementations. Math.sqrt and basic arithmetic are fine (IEEE 754). Verified: ECMAScript lets the others vary by implementation. Implemented: `eslint.config.js`.
3. **Stable lineage-derived IDs** (parent, birth tick, sibling index), so entities stay identifiable across replays, saves and any future forks. Implemented: `sim-core/src/lineage.ts` (64-bit IDs as 16 hex chars, branded `EntityId`). The ID does not encode the parent: the future entity registry must store it and assert uniqueness on registration (not built yet).
4. **Fixed internal substeps for environment fields; schedules never depend on state.**
5. **Causal event log from day one.** Every event records its parent-cause IDs (max 3). Implemented: `sim-core/src/event-log.ts` (append-only, ticks non-decreasing, no event taxonomy yet).
6. **Snapshots are keyframes + command log + replay.** Scrubbing backward is a real system with memory cost, not free.
7. **All tunables in a versioned config;** saves record the config hash. Implemented: `sim-core/src/config.ts` (`hashConfig`, golden-value test). Fields are placeholders (`seasonsPerYear`, `startingBandSize`) until the slice spec names the real tunables.

Implementation status (repo `CONTEXT.md`, checked 2026-11-01): rules 1, 2, 3, 4 (tick loop), 5, 7 built with tests; 67 tests + 1 todo pass on Windows. Rule 6 waits for the command log; the entity registry is not built.

## Known risks
- Lifecycle loops (rev 25): dissolve → host over `fissionAt` → split back into the drought churned in the prototype until the fission guard was added; other loops may exist. Check for new bands dissolving within 8 seasons of founding.
- Dissolution cascades (rev 25): an overloaded host goes hungry and dissolves in turn. Seen in the R4 prototype as a ~150-yr boom-bust on filled maps; the host guard (rev 26) removed it.
- Bands at the food limit (rev 26): with the host guard, filled maps settle through background hunger (3-10% of band-seasons) and bands grow past `fissionAt` when no free site exists. Population regulation is R4b.
- Overlapping home ranges (rev 27, rev 34): homes `homeRange` apart overlap, and migrants may now settle inside occupied ranges. Competitor-share scoring discounts crowded targets, but post-drought crowding in refuges is unmeasured. Migration stays rare (2-9 per drought among ~30 bands): most bands never reach a 2-season hungry streak because plants backfill.
- Migration scoring cost (rev 34): each candidate cell looks up overlapping residents; needs the spatial hash at scale.
- Effort coupling (rev 27): fertility depends on the per-worker return anchor (R4c); at rev 23 values effort on fresh land was ~0.8, which would suppress births everywhere.
- Anchor coupling (rev 28): the return anchor moves with `effortFull` and the Kveg breakpoints; rich land overshoots density (1.6/cell at K4) unless the capacity curve keeps it rare. Rev 41: the calibrated constant goes stale if worldgen or fauna tunables change without rerunning the tool (the slow test guards it); seeds with poor median land sit on the steep part of the effort curve (0.75 at K1 vs 0.50 at K1.5) and may stall births [UNCERTAIN: seed spread unmeasured].
- Perishing rare (rev 33, rev 35): cut-off bands shrink to the 12-24 people the land still feeds; perishing needs a near-total food failure (prototype: capacity floor ≤ 0.1). Die-out relies on dissolution, so Q14's scenario needs a band that is cut off from migration targets but has a host. Small bands (5-15) persist for centuries while fusion is deferred. Calm-time perishing (0-1 per 200 yr across 30 bands) has no hunger cause; rev 36 emits it as outcome `dwindled` with no causes.
- Slow fill (rev 32): calibrated demography fills a 2-band start in ~700 yr (was ~400 at rev 30 prototype rates), and density at equilibrium moves with demographic rates through effort regulation (K2 ~0.50, near the 0.45 floor). Start-state band count and the R4c anchor may need revisiting in batch tuning.
- Bracket age distortion (rev 32): with one aging stage per bracket, some "young adults" are 6 and some "elders" 28. Harmless while no system reads true age; techniques (elders lengthen τ) will.
- Short prototype runs (rev 26): the rev 25 baseline was a growth-phase snapshot. Lifecycle checks must run on a filled map (≥ 600 yr in the prototype).
- Cause attribution by proximity (rev 36): the `causeWindow` lookback cites a drought for any failure of a recently exposed band, including one its own foraging caused; aftermath past the window and crowding of unexposed refuge hosts get no cause. Watch causeless and window-edge failures in batch runs.
- Sequential foraging order (rev 44): bands forage in band ID order on live stocks, so lower IDs eat first where catchments overlap. Watch in batch runs whether outcomes correlate with band ID.
- Natural dry spells untested (rev 45): no prototype ran with volatility > 0; the first batch runs with natural climate must check causeless `forage_failed` and dry-spell share.
- Camp jitter (rev 24): without the move margin the prototype camp moved every season; keep the margin and the check.
- Hunt cap deciding outcomes (rev 23): the per-season take cap is a balancing knob; keep it inside the sustainable-offtake band and watch whether outcomes swing on it in batch runs. Rev 29: the cap ignores herd condition, so in drought hunters take a larger share of real production and may deepen the crash; meat share sits at the top of its target (49%).
- Game extinction once other food feeds people (prompt 11; VEP, Alroy): the take cap guards the slice; farming needs the protein/fat budget or prey depression.
- Fauna boom-bust (rev 22): Type II grazing cycles on rich land without density mortality (prototype-verified); keep the no-drought stability test in the suite.
- Feedback loops that collapse or explode under tuning: invariant tests (no negative biomass, population bounded by carrying capacity).
- Teleology: progress rules that quietly force development. Crucible bias must stay a bias.
- Bimodal tech outcomes: no band ever farms, or all farm at once. Tune with batch runs.
- Arbitrariness: with no Earth anchor, rules can drift toward dice rolls. Keep mechanisms plausible; every event needs recorded causes.
- Legends readability over thousands of years: templated narration and summarization.
- Frontier stall: sim throughput falling below playback in dense eras.
- Search/pathfinding cost (Ultima Online lesson): spatial hashing, budgeted per-tick work.
- Silent cross-browser divergence: CI state-hash check on two engines.
- Log and keyframe memory blow-up: keyframe sparsely; regime-threshold emission keeps the log small.
- World generation growing into its own project.
- Third-party skill/plugin supply-chain risk (tooling report): vet before install; prefer own determinism skill/hooks.
- Die-out leaning on the radius gap (rev 38): 3 of 8 prototype dissolution hosts lay between `searchRadius` (14) and `hostRange` (15), where a host can't also be a migration target. Retuning either radius may change the die-out rate and the Q14 pass rate; do not retune radii to make the test pass. Watch the 20-seed die-out rate.
- `dwindled` wording (rev 38, prompt 12 review): real bands below ~8-12 people merge rather than perish, so `dwindled` is an artifact of deferred fusion; the Legends should say the band dwindled and broke up, not that it starved.
